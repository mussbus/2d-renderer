1. CURRENT OUTPUT IS A FAILURE

Treat the current Prompt 25 rendered character output as a failed visual baseline, not as an acceptable reference to preserve.

Observed failures include, but are not limited to:

obvious gaps between body segments;
detached-looking upper arms, forearms, hands, thighs, lower legs, and feet;
floating feet;
highly visible shoulder, elbow, hip, knee, and ankle segmentation;
limbs that visually resemble sticks or disconnected paper-doll pieces;
inconsistent anatomical proportions between head, torso, arms, pelvis, and legs;
poor transitions between torso and pelvis;
poor transitions between pelvis and legs;
poor transitions between limbs and joints;
clothing that looks pasted onto independent body pieces rather than worn by a continuous body;
inconsistent silhouette quality;
awkward component overlaps;
exposed implementation seams;
inconsistent apparent pixel/detail density between body regions;
permanent modesty/base-presentation regions that read as crude censor rectangles or placeholders;
rigid presentation that unnecessarily emphasizes segmentation;
generally unfinished character-art quality.

Do not rationalize these defects as consequences of a modular renderer.

The purpose of the renderer is to hide those implementation details in the final composition.

2. NON-NEGOTIABLE VISUAL PRINCIPLE

The required invariant is:

MODULAR INTERNALLY. CONTINUOUS EXTERNALLY.

An arm may consist internally of shoulder/upper-arm/forearm/hand components.

The rendered character must still look like one arm.

A leg may consist internally of thigh/lower-leg/foot components.

The rendered character must still look like one leg.

An outfit may consist internally of torso pieces, repeated sleeves, legwear, footwear, accessories, and outer layers.

The rendered character must still look like one person wearing one coherent outfit.

At intended gameplay presentation scale, a viewer who knows nothing about the implementation should not be able to identify the component boundaries merely by looking at the character.

Prompt 24A's anatomy/equipment separation and Prompt 25's reusable wardrobe architecture are requirements.

Fix the visual integration of that architecture.
